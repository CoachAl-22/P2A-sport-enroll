name: Admin account identity
description: A confirmed admin recovery and the account-identity mistake it exposed
---

Do not conflate a retired fictional admin login with a separate branded admin account just because their labels and domains look similar. The user confirmed that the recovered personal admin login worked, then showed a different active branded admin on the live Staff page.

**Why:** Treating those accounts as the same led to an incorrect claim that the visible staff member was already inactive. The user later confirmed that making the separate branded admin inactive through Staff Management fixed the issue while their own admin login remained active. Historical links are better preserved by deactivating an account than deleting its row.

**How to apply:** Before changing staff status or interpreting a failed deletion, verify the exact live account identifier and linked-record state. Never deactivate the administrator's own working login when retiring another account.
