Photo and video permission

Power2ADAPT · version 1 November 2026

Use this form with Coach Al if you booked through Setmore, SportsBiz or another route that did not record these separate choices. Completing or printing this page does not send or save a consent record. Give the completed form to Coach Al to record securely before any recording or use.

Your optional choices

Unticked means no permission. Saying no does not affect participation.

Private coaching permission is not publicity permission. Print promotions, paid advertising, AI-tool use or a new purpose need separate permission.

Parent or guardian / adult athlete

For an athlete under 18, a parent or legal guardian records these choices and the athlete must also agree. Adult athletes record their own choices.

Athlete’s agreement

The coach explains what will be recorded, who will see it and why, in a way the athlete understands. The athlete may say no or stop. Parent permission does not override the athlete’s refusal.

Athlete’s name / agreement recorded by coach, date and permitted purpose

Storage, sharing and changing permission

Private videos are kept in athlete-specific Google Drive folders currently accessed only by Coach Al. Feedback copies are sent to parents through iMessage or WhatsApp. Contact info@power2adapt.com to change or withdraw permission, request removal or ask about retention. Copies saved or shared by other people may be outside Power2ADAPT’s control.
